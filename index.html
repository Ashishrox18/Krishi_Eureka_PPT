No affordable, decentralised treatment solution exists for smallholder farms. Existing industrial solutions are cost-prohibitive at the farm level. Source: FAO SOFA 2020.
The Solution
Four Integrated Layers. One Platform.

Krishi Era is a neutral intelligence layer — not an asset owner — connecting the entire rural ecosystem through AI, IoT, and biotechnology.

IoT Water Intelligence
ESP32 microcontroller + 6-parameter sensor array (COD, BOD, pH, Turbidity, Conductivity, TSS). Real-time water quality monitoring with mobile alert system. BOM cost: ~₹1,350/unit.
Recombinant Microalgae Biotech
Proprietary microalgae strains engineered for agricultural wastewater biosorption. Dual-use output: wastewater treatment + algae biomass sold as organic fertiliser at ₹400/kg.
Gemini / Vertex AI Engine
AI-powered crop planning, real-time market price intelligence, automated selling strategy, and demand forecasting. Personalised advisory in 12 regional languages.
7-Stakeholder Marketplace
Farmers, buyers, transporters, storage providers, input suppliers, FPOs, and government — all on one neutral platform. Zero asset ownership. 2–4% transaction commission model.
